Art. 1. — Pursuant to the provisions of
Article 64 of Law No. 83-11 of 2 July 1983,
The purpose of this Order is to set the conditions for
operation of the medical examination of the insured persons.
Art. 2. — Medical examination consists of giving
advice on medical prescriptions and procedures relating to
the health or working capacity of the beneficiaries
of social security, taking into account their
medical justification and entitlement to social insurance benefits, employment injuries and
occupational diseases as defined by the
legislation and regulations in force .
Art. 3. — Employment injuries and diseases
medical examination covers:
— the cause and effect relationship between the described lesions
and the accident at work, in particular when the accident has
resulted in or is likely to result in death or
permanent incapacity;
— recognition and repair of a condition in
title of the tables of occupational diseases;
— the duration of the work interruption, the date of
healing or consolidation;
— the rate of permanent partial disability
in accordance with the applicable accident schedule of the
work and occupational diseases;
—relapse, aggravation and pension revisions.
Art. 4. — Medical examination of beneficiaries of the
social security is exercised at the level of the security funds
social.
It is also exercised at the level of establishments and
health facilities under the agreement and
the contracting in accordance with the provisions
current legislation and regulations, including
those provided for by Executive Decree No. 04-101 of 11 Safar
1425 corresponding to 1 April 2004.

beneficiaries is exercised by the caisse consulting practitioner

Art. 6. — During the medical examination, the insured person or
the claimant may be assisted by his attending physician
whose fees remain at his expense.
